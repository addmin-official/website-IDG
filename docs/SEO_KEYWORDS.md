## Brand Usage Guidelines

1. **Always lead with full brand name on first mention:**
   - English: *"Iraq Digital Gateway (IDG)"*
   - Kurdish: *"دەروازەی دیجیتاڵیی عێراق (IDG)"*
   - Arabic: *"بوابة العراق الرقمية (IDG)"*
2. **Disambiguation rule:**
   - In international press releases, corporate bios, and investor decks, include the explicit disambiguation statement:
     *"Iraq Digital Gateway (IDG) is an independent Iraqi sovereign technology institution and is not affiliated with International Data Group (IDG Inc.) or IDG Communications."*
3. **Headquarters reinforcement:**
   - Always reference *"Headquartered in Erbil, Kurdistan Region, Iraq"* to reinforce local geographical authority for search algorithms.
